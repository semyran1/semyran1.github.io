# STYLEPACK - Wong Kar-wai / *In the Mood for Love* - *Chungking Express*

Built to `_contract.md`. Load with `wan3-film` and `wan3-core`, and with no other pack. All
`[CRAFT]`. One official sign that the register works on Wan: handbook prompt 063 closes a
retro-garden-to-rainy-car sequence with `Wong Kar-wai style cinematic feel, deep and fluid`
`[DOC]` - a bare name there, so it is evidence of recognition, not of this pack's vocabulary.
Run one 480p 5 s test before relying on the block. Read the whole pack.

## IDENTITY

Longing in saturated colour. Fragments of a day seen through obstructions, bodies that almost
touch, time that stutters and smears at the moment that matters, and music that tells you what the
characters cannot. The thesis is feeling as texture - the memory of a moment, not the moment.

## VOCABULARY

- **Palette:** saturated lipstick red, jade and emerald green, sodium amber, with deep teal-black
  shadows; neon and tungsten colours spill onto skin and walls; one dominant hue per scene.
- **Light:** practical and neon spill, a lamp behind a curtain, light through blinds or a
  rain-streaked window, reflections in mirrors, glass and wet pavement; faces half-lit, half in
  colour.
- **Motion:** step-printed look - the key figure moves in slow, stuttering, smeared motion
  (low shutter drag, the motion leaves trails) while the background stays at normal speed or
  blurs in ribbons.
- **Lens:** wide-angle intimacy at short distance, shallow focus; framing through doorways,
  window grids, foreground obstructions (a doorframe, a hanging clothes line, a bead curtain).
- **Grain:** visible 35 mm grain, halation glow around neon and lamps.
- **Composition:** characters off-centre, pushed to the edge of narrow frames, much of the frame
  taken by wall or obstruction; clock faces, calendars, cigarette smoke, steam.
- **Materials:** wet pavement, tiled walls, fluorescent shop interiors, silk, steam, smoke.

## ASSET BRIEF (-> lira-image-prompts)

**Locations:** narrow interiors (stairwell, noodle stall, corridor) with practical lamps in plate,
colour-neutral; no neon spill baked. **Characters:** neutral sheet, period costume (cheongsam or
1990s office), no grade, no grain. **Props:** clocks, cups, receipts - plain.

## VIDEO STYLE BLOCK (-> wan3-core)

```text
Photoreal 35mm romantic drama, saturated lipstick red, jade green and sodium amber against deep
teal-black shadows; one dominant hue per scene. Neon, tungsten lamps and light through a
rain-streaked window spill onto faces and walls; visible film grain and soft halation around the
lights. Handheld, close and intimate, wide-angle at short distance with shallow focus, framed
through a doorway or foreground obstruction, the subject off-centre in a narrow frame. At the key
gesture the subject moves in slow, stuttering, smeared motion, trailing light, while the
surroundings move at normal speed.
```

## CAMERA DISCIPLINE

**Favours:** handheld close observation; slow lateral drift past an obstruction; a held two-shot
through a doorway; step-printed slow motion for one gesture only; mirror and reflection framing.

**Declines:** clean gimbal glide, drone, symmetrical tableau, wide establishing grandeur, fast
action coverage.

**CUT STATE:** elliptical cuts - a repeated gesture from another angle, a jump forward in time on
an object (a clock, a cup), hard cuts on music; or one handheld take written with `No cut.`

## BLOCKING / MONTAGE

- Two people share a frame but not a look; a passing in a narrow corridor is the central event.
- The gesture is repeated (stirring a cup, lighting a cigarette) in different light = the passing of time.
- Cut on a sound or a lyric; voice-over replaces explanation.

## SOUND

A looping romantic piece (strings, a slow Latin or jazz groove, a plaintive pop song) carries the
scene; ambient street, rain and shop sound underneath; a quiet first-person voice-over lines, if
any, spoken softly and without performance. Paste: `Sound: a slow looping string melody under
street rain and distant traffic; a quiet reflective voice-over, soft and unhurried; no other
dialogue.` (Voice language: `wan3-sound`, speech-language caution.)

## SELF-CHECK

1. Is the frame narrow or obstructed, the subject off-centre, with one dominant saturated hue?
2. Is the slow, smeared motion limited to the one key gesture while the surroundings stay normal?
3. Does music carry the emotion (not dialogue), and are cuts elliptical or on the music?
