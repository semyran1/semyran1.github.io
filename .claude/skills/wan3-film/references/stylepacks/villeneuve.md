# STYLEPACK - Villeneuve / *Arrival* - *Blade Runner 2049* - *Dune*

Built to `_contract.md`. Load with `wan3-film` and `wan3-core`, and with no other pack. All
`[CRAFT]` and untested on Wan 3: run one 480p 5 s test (5 credits) before relying on it. Read the
whole pack.

## IDENTITY

Scale and silence. A human figure inside a structure that does not care about them; light that is
diffused and atmospheric rather than dramatic; a camera that moves slowly and inevitably, and a
sound design that is felt in the chest before it is heard. The thesis is awe by understatement -
nothing hurries, nothing explains.

## VOCABULARY

- **Palette:** muted and monochrome-leaning: sand-ochre, fog grey, concrete grey, one rust-orange
  or sodium haze as the only warm field; shadows desaturated blue-grey, never saturated black.
- **Light:** large, soft, diffused sources - overcast sky, sun filtered through dust or fog, a
  single bright slit or window in a dark volume; silhouettes against haze; the sun is a disc seen
  through atmosphere, not a hard key. Atmosphere is the third dimension: fog, dust or smoke in
  layers receding into the depth.
- **Lens:** wide-to-normal, large-format character: depth and layered planes, a figure small in
  frame with the structure kept in sharp detail; shallow focus is used on a face only when the
  scene turns intimate.
- **Grain:** fine, restrained, digital-clean to light 35 mm; never heavy.
- **Composition:** central or near-central symmetry, monolithic shapes, vast negative space, a low
  horizon or none, the figure seen from behind facing the structure; scale is shown by a tiny
  human against a huge surface.
- **Materials:** raw concrete, sand, dust, mist, brushed metal, still dark water.

## ASSET BRIEF (-> lira-image-prompts)

**Locations:** monumental architecture or terrain sheets **with a scale reference** (a figure
or a door) in the plate; matte surfaces; no people performing; no baked fog or grade - haze and
colour belong to the video prompt. **Characters:** neutral sheet, plain even light, practical
period-neutral costume in muted cloth. **Props:** matte, understated, no logos.

## VIDEO STYLE BLOCK (-> wan3-core)

```text
Photoreal monumental science-fiction drama. Muted palette of sand ochre, fog grey and concrete
grey with one rust-orange haze as the only warm field; shadows desaturated blue-grey. Light is
large and diffused: overcast sky and sun seen through dust and haze, silhouettes against the haze,
atmosphere rendered in receding layers. Wide-to-normal lens in a large-format look, deep layered
focus with the structure kept in detail; a single small human figure in a vast frame, central
symmetry and generous empty space. Fine restrained film grain. Everything moves slowly.
```

## CAMERA DISCIPLINE

**Favours:** slow, smooth, inevitable movement - a gliding dolly or crane, a very slow push-in
toward a monolith, a track behind a walking figure that keeps the scale visible, a slow rise to
reveal the size of the space; long takes.

**Declines:** handheld shake, whip pans, snap zooms, fast orbit, shaky action coverage, rapid
cutting. Every move has one named subject and a named end (`the camera rises slowly until the
whole canyon and the lone figure are in frame`).

**CUT STATE:** few cuts; each one hard, on a sound or on a completed action, or a slow dissolve
through haze; most scenes are one take written with `No cut.`

## BLOCKING / MONTAGE

- The figure is shown from behind or in profile, facing the structure; the distance to it is the
  drama.
- Two people stand apart in a wide shot, a long silence between lines; a close-up is a rare reward.
- Scale cues in every wide: a door, a vehicle, a second distant figure.
- Montage by contrast of scale: wide structure -> small hand or eye -> wide again.

## SOUND

A low-frequency drone or sub-bass pulse under everything, sparse metallic or glassy textures,
wind through the structure; dialogue sparse, quiet, close, never overlapped; silence before an
event. Paste: `Sound: a deep sub-bass drone, wind and a few sparse metallic resonances; no melody; no dialogue.` (Add a spoken line only when the brief supplies it: close, dry, quiet.)

## SELF-CHECK

1. Is the human figure small in a layered, atmospheric frame, with a scale cue present?
2. Is every camera move slow, smooth, named with its subject and end, and free of shake?
3. Is the light diffused (haze, overcast, dust) rather than a hard dramatic key, and does the sound
   carry low-frequency weight rather than melody?
