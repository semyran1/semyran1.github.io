# STYLEPACK - Wes Anderson / *The Grand Budapest Hotel*

Built to `_contract.md`. Load with `wan3-film` and `wan3-core`, and with no other pack. All
`[CRAFT]`. Related official evidence `[DOC]`: handbook prompt 017 builds a luxury hotel corridor
where `the arches, wall lamps, and carpet patterns in the background are all strictly symmetrical
left and right`, freezes two cats in a `dynamic stillness, like exquisite clockwork toys`, then cuts
to a `profile perspective of the corridor (as if the wall has been cut open)` - the same
symmetry-then-lateral grammar. Run one 480p 5 s test before relying on the block. Read the whole
pack.

## IDENTITY

The world as a diorama. Frontal, symmetrical, pastel, exact: every object placed, every movement
a straight line, every face deadpan. The thesis is control as charm - a precisely arranged world in
which strong feeling is delivered in the flattest possible voice.

## VOCABULARY

- **Palette:** pastel pink, powder blue, mustard yellow, mint, cream, with one saturated
  burgundy or red accent; flat, even colour fields, painted-set materials.
- **Light:** soft, flat, even and frontal, no hard shadows, no visible dramatic source; daylight
  like a stage in a miniature.
- **Lens:** normal to moderately wide, deep focus, planimetric view - the camera square to the
  back wall; no distortion tricks.
- **Composition:** strict left-right symmetry, the subject on the vertical axis, props arranged in
  rows and grids, signage and titles in one consistent serif or typewriter typeface; staged like a
  tableau with the characters facing the lens.
- **Materials:** painted wood, felt, paper, miniatures, uniforms with brass buttons.
- **Grain:** light, clean, 35 mm daylight stock look.

## ASSET BRIEF (-> lira-image-prompts)

**Locations:** square-on, perfectly symmetrical sheets, one pastel palette, props in order, no
people; no grain, no vignette baked. **Characters:** front-facing neutral sheet, deadpan, period or
uniform costume with exact colours. **Props:** isolated, evenly lit, centred, in grid sheets.

## VIDEO STYLE BLOCK (-> wan3-core)

```text
Live-action storybook comedy-drama in a precisely arranged diorama world. Strictly symmetrical,
planimetric composition, the camera square to the back wall, subjects centred on the vertical axis
and facing the lens. Pastel palette of pink, powder blue, mustard and mint with one burgundy
accent; flat, even, frontal daylight, no hard shadows. Normal-to-wide lens, deep focus, props
arranged in neat rows. Deadpan faces, movements in straight lines. Light, clean film grain.
```

## CAMERA DISCIPLINE

**Favours:** a dead-static frame; a lateral track at constant speed along a row of rooms (the
dollhouse cutaway); a 90-degree whip pan between two symmetrical frames; a straight-on zoom; an
overhead top-down on hands and objects.

**Declines:** handheld, dutch angles, shallow depth of field, shaky or dynamic coverage, a camera
that peeks from the side.

**CUT STATE:** hard cuts on the beat of the music and on completed actions; whip-pan transitions;
insert cutaways of objects on a plain background; chapter title cards. Each declared.

## BLOCKING / MONTAGE

- Characters stand in a line or a triangle facing the camera and speak to it.
- Movement is a straight line at a steady pace, in one plane.
- Scenes are built as a chain of symmetrical tableaux, linked by a lateral move or a whip.
- Rhythm is metronomic, comic through exactness.

## SOUND

Precise foley (a stamp, a door latch, paper) loud and dry; a whimsical score of plucked strings,
mandolin, harpsichord or a marching snare; dialogue fast, flat, in a deadpan voice; a deadpan
voice-over narrator may link chapters. Paste: `Sound: crisp dry foley, a light plucked-string and
harpsichord score on the beat, deadpan fast flat dialogue.`

## SELF-CHECK

1. Is the frame symmetrical and planimetric, the camera square to the wall, with the subject on the
   vertical axis?
2. Is the palette limited to pastels with one accent, and the light flat and frontal?
3. Is every move straight (lateral track, whip, zoom), with the cut state declared and no handheld?
